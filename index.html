My interests span Bayesian statistics, generalized linear models, multivariate methods, machine learning, forensic statistics, and spatial statistics.

FEATURED RESEARCH · IOWA STATE UNIVERSITY · 2024

Assessing the strength of cartridge case evidence

For my master's creative component, I developed and implemented a two-stage approach, score-based likelihood ratios, and Bayes factors to assess whether a pair of cartridge cases was fired from the same firearm. I used R for computationally intensive data analysis.

Forensic statisticsBayesian methodsR
UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · KNUST · 2020–2021

Modeling hostel accommodation rates

Used multiple linear regression to investigate factors associated with off-campus hostel rates around KNUST, with data collected through stratified random sampling.

Selected coursework projects +
  • US fruit and vegetable acreage · 2025

    Generalized linear models, composite likelihood, bootstrap methods, and time series to evaluate production acreage over time.

  • In-vehicle coupon utilization · 2024

    Prediction using K-nearest neighbors, linear discriminant analysis, random forests, and AdaBoost.

  • House prices and square footage · 2023

    Bayesian inference to study the relationship between sale price and square footage.
